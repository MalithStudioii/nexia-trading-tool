Local Processing: We respect your privacy. Nexia Pro does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, wallet addresses, or trade inputs to any external servers. All journal exports, risk plans, and saved trade history (via LocalStorage) are processed and stored securely and locally inside your browser.
